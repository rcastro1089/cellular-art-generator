# Copywriting & Language Analysis

## Source: DuckDuckGo search results, Reddit post titles/descriptions (2026)

## How People Talk About CA Art

### Language from Search Queries
- "make art from code"
- "cool patterns"
- "poster for my room"
- "give as gift to programmer friend"
- "made this, came out awesome"
- "frame it and hang it"
- "math meets art"
- "unique, one of a kind"

### Language that Does NOT Appear (but technical people use)
- "algorithmic art" (only 390 searches)
- "procedural generation"
- "emergent behavior"
- "cellular automata" (2,900 searches — mostly technical)

## The 5 Most Powerful Copy Angles

1. **"Create your own unique math art"** — personal + unique + mathematical
2. **"The perfect gift for programmers who have everything"** — niche + solution
3. **"Turn code into wall art"** — simple, powerful, visual
4. **"Generative art you design yourself"** — control + creativity
5. **"From algorithm to canvas"** — narrative of transformation

## Recommended Brand Voice

| Tone | Avoid |
|------|-------|
| Playful ("play with rules") | Technical ("configure B/S parameters") |
| Visual ("watch patterns emerge") | Dry ("cellular automaton simulation") |
| Personal ("your creation") | Impersonal ("the algorithm generates") |
| Accessible ("no coding needed") | Elitist ("for programmers only") |

## Key Phrases for H1 / Meta

- *"Design your own cellular automata art. Pick the rules, find the perfect frame, and turn it into a poster, canvas, or t-shirt."*
- *"From algorithm to wall art in one click."*
- *"Generative art isn't just for programmers anymore."*

## Content Pillars to Establish

1. **"Algo-art"** — coin the term, define the category
2. **"Algorithmic art for walls"** — position as decor, not toy
3. **"From code to canvas in one click"** — value proposition
