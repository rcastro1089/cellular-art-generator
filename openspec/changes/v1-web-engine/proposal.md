# Proposal: Cellular Automata Art Generator + Print-on-Demand

## Why This Project?

We have identified a market gap: **there is no tool that combines a powerful generative art engine (cellular automata) with integrated print-on-demand, targeting the scientific/math/decorative art niche.**

### Problem

1. **Existing CA simulators** (playgameoflife.com, cellular-automata.studio, Golly) are purely technical tools — no export, no monetization, no focus on visual quality.
2. **Existing generative art stores** (cognitive-surplus.com, atlasautomata.art) sell *curated* prints at $108-122, but the user cannot create their own design.
3. **AI wall art generators** (Wallnora, Artsire, Mew Design) are generic — they don't leverage the unique mathematical beauty of cellular automata.
4. **No competitor connects generation + print in one click** at an accessible price point.

### Opportunity

- **Search demand:** "cellular automata" (2,900/mo, ↗️), "algorithmic art" (390/mo, CPC $16.52), "game of life simulator" (320/mo), "scientific illustration" (3,600/mo, CPC $6.48)
- **Low competition:** "cellular automata poster", "conway game of life art", "custom algorithm art" have ZERO search volume data — meaning nobody is targeting them
- **Proven willingness to pay:** Cognitive Surplus sells science art at $20-60; atlasautomata sells CA art at $108-122
- **Our differentiator:** The user creates their OWN art by playing with rules, then exports/orders it

### Capabilities Required

1. **C1 — WebGL2 GPU Engine:** Fast, beautiful CA simulation with shaders, supporting gradients, glow, and high resolution
2. **C2 — Surface Modes:** Planar, toroidal, spherical, and (optionally) hyperbolic CA surfaces
3. **C3 — 3D Mode:** Three.js-based 3D CA visualization with orbit controls
4. **C4 — HD Export:** Render current frame at 4000×4000+ px for print-quality download
5. **C5 — Print-On-Demand Integration:** Printful API integration for posters, canvases, mugs, t-shirts
6. **C6 — Content & SEO:** Blog, gallery, pattern showcase pages for organic traffic
7. **C7 — Monetization:** Digital download sales ($9-19) + POD commissions ($12-25)

### Impact

- **User:** Gets a free, beautiful generative art tool + ability to own physical prints of their creations
- **Creator:** Passive income from digital downloads + POD commissions
- **Market:** First-mover in the "generative CA art print" space
